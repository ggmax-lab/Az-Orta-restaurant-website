2. Personal Data Processed

Our website (azorta.com) does not actively collect personal data from its visitors. The only element to be considered in this context is the optionally loaded map service:

The limited data processing activity described above (the optional loading of Google Maps) is carried out solely to show the location of our restaurant and to provide you with directions, upon the request of the data subject, within the scope of Article 5 of the KVKK.

  • To object to a result arising against you due to the analysis of your data exclusively through automated systems,
