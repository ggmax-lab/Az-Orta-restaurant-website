Our Chef & Founding Story

The Az Orta Story

MasterChef Türkiye 2024 finalist Kerem Bilginer brought his open-fire grill passion to Maslak through Az Orta. His lifelong goal of running his own restaurant and serving the best quality food has become reality through charcoal-grilled flavours.

Read More

House marinades, ocakbaşı tradition, and fresh ingredients shape our skewers and köfte. Az Orta is built around a simple goal: a warm fire-side table with memorable food and sincere hospitality.
